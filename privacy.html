What is not collected

No payment card data: payments are handled by the payment provider you use, never by this site. No location tracking, no contact list access, no biometric data.

How the brief form works

The form on the home page does not send data to a server. It opens your own email client with the content filled in, and nothing leaves your device until you press send in that client. From then on the message is handled as email.

Who it is shared with

Not sold, not rented, not traded. Shared only with the providers needed to run the work, and only as far as needed:

  • The hosting provider that serves this site, and the code host that stores it.
  • The email provider that carries correspondence.
  • Model and document-processing providers used to turn a brief into drawings and documents.

Shared further only where the law requires it, or where you ask us to.
