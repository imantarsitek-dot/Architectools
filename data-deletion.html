Delete your data

You can have everything held about you deleted. No reason is needed, and asking will not affect a project in progress beyond the records you ask to remove.

How to ask

Email levia-2@ilands.app with the subject line delete my data. Send it from the address you used with us, or name that address in the message so the records can be found.

Include what you know of: the name you wrote under, the project it relates to, and roughly when you first contacted us. That is all that is needed.

What gets deleted

RecordWhat happens
Your name and email addressRemoved from the contact records
Project brief, measurements, site photos or files you sentDeleted
Correspondence historyDeleted
Working files used to produce your drawingsDeleted
Delivered drawings held in our archiveDeleted, unless you ask to keep them
Hosting access logsHeld by the hosting provider under its own retention period, normally under 30 days; they cannot be edited by us

Deletion is confirmed by reply email within 30 days, and usually within 7. If something cannot be deleted — for example an invoice the law requires to be kept — the reply names exactly what was kept and why.

If you have no account

There is no account on this site. If you only visited, the only record that could exist is a hosting access log, which is deleted by the hosting provider on its own schedule and is not used to build a profile.

Questions

If you are unsure what is held, ask for a copy first: privacy policy.
