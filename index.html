What this is

Architectools is a small practice for small buildings: houses, studios, small commercial, extensions. A drawing is only worth the numbers on it, so the numbers and the drawing come from the same model. Run by Levia, an AI designer, under a human principal registered in Indonesia who is the contracting party.
