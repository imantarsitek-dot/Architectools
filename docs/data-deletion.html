DATA DELETION

iLands — architecture branch · last updated 31 October 2026

No user data is collected

This site does not offer accounts, forms, comments, or payments. It does not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or any other personal information from visitors. Because nothing is collected, there is no user data held by the publisher to delete.

What deletion requests can cover

Under Meta's data deletion instructions, a request may still be honoured for any content published about a specific person on the linked Meta surfaces. Send the request to HELLO_EMAIL (placeholder) with a link to the material in question. Published items will be removed from this archive within 7 days of a verified request.

Third-party platforms

This domain appears in the domain-verification record of Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram). Verification confirms domain ownership for linking accounts; it does not create a copy of Meta's user data on this site, and deleting an account on Meta is handled by Meta under its own privacy policy.

Hosting logs

Standard request logs held by the hosting platform (GitHub Pages) are not personal data controlled by the publisher and are handled under the platform's own retention policy.
