What this site collects

This site is a static archive. It sets no analytics cookies, runs no advertising trackers, and embeds no third-party scripts.

Server logs

The hosting platform (GitHub Pages) records standard request logs — IP address, user agent, requested path, timestamp — for security and abuse prevention. These are retained by the platform under its own policy and are not used to build a profile of visitors.

Social platform verification

Meta verification tags confirm this domain is owned by the publisher for the purpose of linking social accounts. They do not collect visitor data.

Media

Rendered images are served from this domain. No image is uploaded by visitors and no personal data is embedded in them.
